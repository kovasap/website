# Everyday Gear

A collection of stuff that I like to use on a daily basis.
Many of these items were hard to find; I tried many options before settling on
these.

## Head

 - Bone conduction wireless headphones, $20, https://amzn.to/4jJUAZl
 - Sunglasses, $18, https://amzn.to/3WcWUyh.  Also free swag sunglasses work pretty well.
 - Sun hat, $15, https://amzn.to/4hR7pPe.  Not used that much.
